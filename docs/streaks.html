Nobody is due

Can we tell which players are about to win big after a run of losses, or lose big after a run of wins? We tested it on every carded visit, Jan–Jun 2025. Past results don't predict the next one. What can be predicted is how big a player's swings will be, not which way they'll go.

Why nobody is due

    The tests behind it

    Luck per visit is the actual payout minus the expected payout (coin-in less theo), as on the Players page. Its standard deviation is exact, from each game's payout variance and the bets placed.
