July with no action

The yardstick any action is measured against: a model refit on Jan–Jun with a Jul 4 holiday term, scaled to June's softer level (×). An action has to move daily theo by more than the detectable effect to show up against it.

Where June's shortfall came from

Every model over-forecast June. Locals dipped a little; visitors fell further, in a month when Las Vegas visitation ran below 2024. Ordinary (non-event) days, theo per day.

How a July action would be measured

80% power at a 5% test level over 30 days. The detectable effect is the smallest change the comparison can tell apart from noise.
