The weekly pattern

Floor theo runs on a seven-day cycle: a quiet Tuesday, a busy Saturday, and the same shape again the next week. This page shows how closely it repeats and the tests behind the claim. Every forecast on this site starts from it.

By weekday

Ordinary days only (no event from the events table). The p-value tests whether that weekday's level changed between Jan–Apr and May–Jun (Welch t-test on the log index); q corrects it for testing seven weekdays.

How much today looks like a day k days ago

Autocorrelation of log theo. Spikes every 7 days mean the cycle repeats; the shaded band is what noise alone would give.

Which cycles carry the variation

Periodogram of log theo, linear trend removed. One peak, at seven days, holds most of the day-to-day variation.

Is every weekday different from every other?

Tukey's test on all 21 pairs, month level removed. Each cell is the row day's level against the column day's; the stronger the green, the smaller the p-value. A grey pair would be indistinguishable.

Does each month repeat the same week?

Correlation between months' weekday profiles (7 points each). Values near 1 mean the same shape, whatever the level.

The tests behind it

Days of a listed event are left out unless noted (they are spikes, not the pattern). Daily data are autocorrelated, so treat tiny p-values as "far beyond chance", not as exact.
