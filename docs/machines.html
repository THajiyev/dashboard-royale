Luck ranking

Luck is house win minus theo, the win the game's payout rate predicts for the amount played. A fair machine lands within ±1.96 standard deviations 95% of the time, so about one machine in twenty looks unusual by chance alone. One machine stands out because its payouts don't match its configuration.

Flagged machines

Flagged: payout rate off its configuration, or luck beyond chance, after correcting for testing every machine. Watch: luck outside the 99% range, a monthly drift alarm, or carded players flagged for win rate who played mostly on it. Across 310 fair machines about four drift alarms happen by chance, so most watch entries are noise worth a second look next month. A flag is a reason to inspect, not proof of anything.
