Key findings

  • 80% of visitors gamble, but only 7.3% come primarily to gamble; gamblers average 2.6 hours per day.
  • This describes all Las Vegas visitors, not Gilded Ace guests — use it for context and segment sizing, not as property data.
  • Household income: $150,000 to $199,999 25%, $120,000 to $149,999 21%.
  • Visitor age: 30 to 39 35%, 40 to 49 33%.
  • Visitor behaviors: Visitor Satisfaction - Very Satisfied 87%, Gambled During Visit 80%.
  • Visitor origin: Western states 57%, California 32%.
  • Spending (USD): Total Gaming Budget and Non-Gaming Expenditures $2,130, Total Non-Gaming Expenditures per Trip $1,447, Gambling Budget (among those who gambled) $849, Gambling Budget (among all visitors including those that spent $0) $683.
