Key findings

  • Play follows a strong daily cycle: occupancy runs from 7.0% at 5:00 to 64.7% at 20:00.
  • Carded share varies by hour (64.4%–69.0%): late-night play is more carded than the evening peak.
  • Friday and Saturday evenings are the busiest cells in the week; weekday early mornings the emptiest — natural windows for maintenance and moves.
  • Sums to machine_day; the 23-hour DST gaming day (Mar 8) has one fewer hour per machine.
