Key findings

  • Hold depends on the denomination group, not simply on size: among major categories (≥2% of handle) 1 cent 10.9%, Multi Denom 10.8%, 10 cents 9.2%, $1.00 6.7%, 5 cents 6.5%, 25 cents 6.0%. Small high-denomination categories swing widely on little handle. Gilded Ace's 9.5% sits between the high-hold penny games and the ~6–7% nickel/quarter/dollar games.
  • 1 cent is the dominant category by handle (62%).
  • Largest property by handle: ameristar_st_charles ($1.3B); property hold ranges 9.1%–10.9%.
  • Do not add these rows to benchmark_property_monthly — that file is the published totals of the same casinos.
