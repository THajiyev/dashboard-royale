Key findings

  • Points pile up: only 4.4% of awarded points were redeemed. Balances restart at 0 each year, so most points will expire unused — comps are a light lever today.
  • Redemptions are all F&B comps; the most popular are COMP-SODA, COMP-BEER, COMP-WINE. Economic cost averages 28% of face value.
  • Tier upgrades: Base → Gold 5,253, Gold → Platinum 1,377. Upgrades accumulate through the half-year because tiers use lifetime points.
  • Median award is 154 points per segment; a few large awards (7,125 max) come from long, high-bet sessions.
