Key findings

  • Coin-in lift vs the no-event daily average ($1.3M): Super Bowl LIX weekend +45%, Gilded Ace Winter Slot Tournament +39%, EDC Las Vegas festival weekend +36%, College basketball tournament opening rounds +34%, Gilded Ace Locals Appreciation Week +5%, NAB Show +4%, CES -4%, CinemaCon -10%.

  • Property promotions (Gilded Ace Winter Slot Tournament, Gilded Ace Locals Appreciation Week) are fictional; citywide events have real dates. Most events were announced months ahead (median 350 days), so they can be planned for.
