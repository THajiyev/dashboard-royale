Key findings

  • Each session uses one fixed bet; 83% of sessions bet $1 or less. High-limit bets ($5+) are 2.6% of sessions but 8% of coin-in.
  • Players walk away ahead in 16% of sessions — the house edge shows up in totals, not in most individual sessions.
  • Play peaks in the evening (20:00) and on Friday/Saturday nights; the quietest hour is 5:00.
  • Busiest days: Sat Feb 22 (6,456), Sat Feb 08 (6,418), Sat Mar 22 (6,143).
  • 2,271 logical sessions cross the 06:00 gaming-day boundary and are split into two segments.
