Key findings

  • Real LVCVA data: every month of H1 2025 had fewer visitors than the same month of 2024 — a soft citywide backdrop.
  • Peak visitation per day was 2025-04; June was the weakest, also the month with the lowest convention attendance.
  • Convention attendance swings more than visitors: 374.6K to 628.8K per month.
  • Indexed to January = 100, the property's coin-in per day follows the city's visitors per day loosely; both dip in June. Figures were published after 2025 (as_of_limitation).
