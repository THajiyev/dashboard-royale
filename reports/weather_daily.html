Key findings

  • Temperature rises steadily through the window (a ~25°C increase in average highs from January to June).
  • Rain is rare: 7 days with any measured precipitation, 10 more with only a trace.
  • Weather barely explains play: correlation between daily high and coin-in is -0.03, and -0.06 after removing the weekday effect.
  • These are calendar dates; the gaming day runs 06:00–06:00, so overnight play maps to the previous calendar date.
