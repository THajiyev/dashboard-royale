Key findings

  • Observed hit rate per configuration matches the configured hit probability within ~1 point (e.g. G-PEN89: 65.7% vs 65.7%), so the meters are internally consistent.
  • Negative house-win days are normal: 12.9% of machine-days pay out more than they take in, most often on the very-high-volatility game. By volatility class: low 4%, medium 16%, high 13%, very high 28%.
  • Average bet is stable at $0.78–$0.92 per day; volume (spins), not bet size, drives daily swings.
  • Machines are available 99.62% of scheduled time; seats are occupied 36.4% of available time.
