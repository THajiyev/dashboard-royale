Key findings

  • Center Pit and Bar Row see the most crossings; High-Limit Room the fewest. Counts are crossings, not unique people, and seated players don't trigger sensors.
  • Traffic peaks around 20:00 and bottoms out near 5:00 local; weekends are busier across all hours.
  • Back Forty crossings dropped from 568 to 421 per day (-26%) while its walkway was closed.
  • A missing count is not zero: offline hours have null entries_count. Use coverage_minutes to scale or exclude partial hours.
