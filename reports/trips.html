Key findings

  • Visit frequency is very uneven: 71% of players appear once, 3.6% appear 20+ times and they account for 51% of all visits.
  • Duration is first-to-last carded activity, not arrival-to-departure; median 0.8 h, 5% of visits are a single instant (one F&B or loyalty touch).
  • Visits start most often around 20:00; Saturdays average 2,524 visits vs 1,567 on Tuesdays.
