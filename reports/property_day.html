Key findings

  • Observed hold (9.50%) tracks theoretical hold (9.46%) closely over the half-year; luck nets to $92.8K. Daily hold swings between 6.8% and 11.4%, so single days say little.
  • Demand is strongly weekly: average Saturday coin-in is $1.8M vs $1.1M on Tuesday (+64%). Friday–Sunday carry 51.0% of coin-in on 43% of days.
  • Month-to-month the property is flat: coin-in per day ranges from $1.3M (2025-06) to $1.4M (2025-02).
  • Event days lift coin-in vs the no-event daily average ($1.3M): Super Bowl LIX weekend +45%, Gilded Ace Winter Slot Tournament +39%, EDC Las Vegas festival weekend +36%. Weakest: CinemaCon (-10%) — mid-week conventions bring city crowds, not necessarily players.
  • Floor traffic and play move together (correlation 0.98 between daily zone entries and coin-in); F&B cash adds $2.5M, small next to gaming.
