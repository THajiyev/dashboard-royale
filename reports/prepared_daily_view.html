Key findings

  • Location matters most. Coin-in per machine-day: Main Entrance $5.5K vs Back Forty $2.9K (+87%).
  • Back Forty coin-in per machine-day fell from $3.1K to $2.3K (-25%) while the Back Forty walkway was closed for renovation (Mar 3 – Apr 7); other zones moved +6% over the same weeks. Access paths drive traffic.
  • Observed hold matches configured hold theme by theme (e.g. Jackpot Summit 12.4% observed vs 12.0% theo); low-RTP games are not losing players at the meter level. Best contribution per machine-day: Jackpot Summit ($525); worst: Royal Draw ($195).
  • Owned machines contribute $409 per day vs $345 (fixed lease) and $339 (revenue share) once lease costs are subtracted.
  • Ridgeline machines open 4.0 faults per 100 machine-days vs 1.1 for Halcyon (3.6×), with lower availability (99.32%).
