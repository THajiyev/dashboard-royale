Key findings

  • Gilded Grill takes the most cash ($1.2M from 47,729 tickets); Ace Bar $904.8K from 117,392 tickets, Lantern Cafe $387.6K from 62,712 tickets.
  • Comps are 26% of tickets but zero cash; their cost of goods ($190.7K) is the real expense, not the $682.7K menu value.
  • Highest-margin items: soda 87%, coffee 84%, pastry 75%; lowest: steak 63%.
  • Each outlet keeps its own hours (gaming-day order): Gilded Grill 11:00–23:00, peak 22:00; Ace Bar 10:00–4:00, peak 23:00; Lantern Cafe 6:00–22:00, peak 9:00.
  • One-third of sales (34%) have no player card, so F&B linked to players understates total F&B.
