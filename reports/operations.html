Key findings

  • Fault rate per machine: Ridgeline 7.2, Northwind 3.0, Halcyon 1.9 repairs per machine over six months.
  • Repair downtime is right-skewed: half are fixed within 3.2 h but 10% take longer than 7.9 h — those are the costly ones in lost play.
  • 60% of repairs start the moment the fault opens; the other 40% wait a median 121 min (p90 370 min) for a free technician — the lever behind the A-TECH-SHIFT action.
  • Faults are reported mostly during busy hours (peak at 21:00 local), which is when downtime costs the most.
  • The ten most fault-prone machines account for 135 repairs (11%); M-1241 alone had 16.
  • Non-repair tickets: 19 (installs, moves, maintenance, retirements). The largest single cost is $22.0K (install).
