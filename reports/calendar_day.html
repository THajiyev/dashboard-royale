Key findings

  • A gaming day runs 06:00 → 06:00 local. Its UTC start shifts from 14:00 to 13:00 once daylight saving starts — always join on gaming_date or the UTC bounds, never on calendar date.
  • Gaming date 2025-03-08 has 23 scheduled hours; per-hour metrics must divide by scheduled_seconds.
  • Holidays: New Year's Day (Jan 01), Martin Luther King Jr. Day (Jan 20), Washington's Birthday (Feb 17), Memorial Day (May 26), Juneteenth (Jun 19).
  • Each weekday appears 25–26 times, so weekday averages are directly comparable.
