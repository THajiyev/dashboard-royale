Key findings

  • Value is concentrated: the top 1% of players produce 23% of theo, the top 10% 66%, and the bottom half just 5.9%.

  • 12% of player-months have no carded play at all (F&B or loyalty activity only).
  • Tier movement: 5,253 Base players climbed a tier during the window; comp reinvestment is 0.9% of theo for Base vs 1.4% for Platinum.
  • Monthly active players range 23,304–26,346; March is the busiest month.
