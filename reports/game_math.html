Key findings

  • House edge (1 − RTP) runs from 5% (Royal Draw) to 12% (Jackpot Summit). Higher edge does not mean less play — see prepared_daily_view by theme.
  • Volatility spans orders of magnitude: payout variance 0.99 to 516 (Jackpot Summit). High-variance games need much longer to converge to their theo hold.
  • Hit probability counts any positive payout, including ones smaller than the bet, so a 50% hit rate still loses money on average.
